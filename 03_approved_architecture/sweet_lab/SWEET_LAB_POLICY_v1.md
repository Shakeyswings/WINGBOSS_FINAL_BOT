# Wing⚡Boss Sweet Lab Policy v1

Status: OWNER-APPROVED OPERATING POLICY / PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION; NOT A RELEASE.

## Mandatory fryer isolation
- Dessert fryer(s) are dedicated to sweets.
- Sweet fryer oil must never mix with wing/Boss Mode oil.
- Boss Mode fryer remains savory-only.

## Base dessert direction
- Pancake batter is the common batter platform under development.
- Powdered sugar is an approved base finish direction.

## Finisher development pool
Potential approved-for-testing finishers include caramel drizzle, chocolate drizzle, hot honey, regular honey, whipped cream, fruit-flavored drizzles, and other owner-approved dripping flavors.

## Governance
- Exact recipes, portions, fryer temperatures/times, allergen controls, and selling prices remain PROPOSED until kitchen validated.
- Missing unit costs remain null / NEEDS_COST_INPUT.
- Sweet finishers should be modeled as separate modifier options so recipe cost and contribution can be measured independently.
